## Description (4000)

One for each platform, in the words of the Google Play listing, with the section about the device written for each. The rest is the same text in both, so a change to one usually belongs in the other.

Two things the Play text says that these do not, because they are not true here: widgets and reminders "stay current without opening the app" (iOS has no background refresh yet: a reminder still arrives with the app closed, but a date settled on another device stays until the app is next opened), and automation by Tasker.

### iPhone and iPad (3960)

```
thingGLANCE is for the dates attached to the things you own, the ones nobody reminds you about until it is too late: Is the laptop still under warranty? When does that free trial start charging? How long will the phone keep getting security updates? What are all these subscriptions costing a year?

thingGLANCE answers those questions by showing you, at a glance, what is coming up and what needs a decision from you now.

No accounts. No analytics. No ads. Your data stays on your device and only leaves it when you choose.

Not a home inventory: no serial-number spreadsheets, no receipts to scan. Just the dates that matter and what they cost.

Every date gets a countdown (T-12d), and every thing gets a verdict you can read in one look:
• Prime: plenty of life left
• Solid: nothing to do
• Watch: the end of its window is in sight
• Replace soon, or Past its window

HOW IT WORKS
• Add a thing: a laptop, a phone, an appliance, anything with a warranty or a shelf life.
• Type its name and the web lookup fills in a photo, specs, release date and when security updates end. Optional, and it only sends the name you typed.
• Add its coverage (warranty, extended cover) and the plans that go with it.
• When a date comes close it lands in "Needs a decision". Deal with it, clear it, move on.

WARRANTIES AND SHELF LIFE
• How much of each thing's life is left, as a ring and a bar, with marks where coverage and support end.
• Know a warranty is ending while you can still use it, and when security updates stop.
• The upgrade fund works out what to set aside each month so every replacement is paid for the day it is due.

PLANS AND RENEWALS
• Subscriptions, memberships and service plans: weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly.
• What they cost a month and a year, and what will actually leave your account month by month.
• Trials are flagged before they convert. Mark any plan for review and you are reminded ahead of each renewal, with what you would get back by cancelling.
• The Horizon lays out every date for the year ahead on one timeline.

BUILT FOR IPHONE AND IPAD
• Home Screen widgets: what needs a decision, the next date up, a single thing with its life ring, plan charges for the next 30 days, and quick add. Clear a date with one tap.
• Lock Screen widgets: how many dates are waiting, and the next one counted down.
• Reminders: a heads-up when a date enters its window and one last call closer in. Two at most per date. Clear one with a single "Done with this".
• Siri and Shortcuts: ask what is next, settle a date or add a thing.
• Add a thing from Control Center (iOS 18 and later), or with a long press on the app icon.
• Share a product page from Safari or any other app to start a new thing.

BUILT FOR A HOUSEHOLD
• Say who each thing belongs to, who pays for each plan, and who deals with each date.
• Reminders go to the person who deals with it, not to everyone.
• Keep an item private and only its owner sees it, on any device.

YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE
• Works fully offline. Export a backup file and import it whenever you like.
• Sync on your terms with GLANCEvault, a sync server you host. Everything is end-to-end encrypted with a key made from your passphrase, so the server holds only what it cannot read.
• Connects to other GLANCE apps: send a date to dayGLANCE as a task, and the date clears itself here when the task is done. Through your own vault, and off by default.
• Try it with sample data first. Samples never sync, and editing one makes it yours.
• Open source, and the web app can be self-hosted under Docker if you would rather run it yourself.

ONE PRICE, ONCE
thingGLANCE is a one-time purchase, and one purchase covers your iPhone, iPad and Mac. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no paywall inside the app.

Terms of Use: https://www.glance-apps.com/eula
Privacy Policy: https://www.glance-apps.com/thingglance/privacy

No inventory to keep up. Just an honest picture of what is next for your things.
```

## App Privacy

**Data Not Collected.** Nothing the app handles reaches the developer: there is no account, no analytics and no crash reporting, and a vault is the user's own server. [The privacy policy](https://www.glance-apps.com/thingglance/privacy) has the detail, and `docs/release.md` ("Where data goes") is the same statement for Google.

## Export compliance

`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is `false` in every Info.plist, so App Store Connect does not ask. The app uses only standard algorithms (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2-SHA-256, HMAC-SHA-256) for end-to-end sync. The annual self-classification report is filed separately, as for the sibling apps.

## Notes for App Review

```
thingGLANCE needs no account and no sign-in. Everything works on first launch: choose "Explore with sample data" on the welcome screen to see every screen filled in.

Sync is optional and uses GLANCEvault, a server the user runs themselves; none is needed to review the app.

Notifications are local reminders scheduled on the device. The widgets and the share extension read and write only the app's own App Group.

(Mac) The app requests com.apple.security.network.server because two optional features, both off by default, listen on 127.0.0.1 only: a local server that lets an AI assistant on the same Mac read the user's data (Settings, Integrations, On this computer), and a listener for the Stream Deck plugin. Neither is reachable from the network. A short video of both is attached.
```

The video is yours to record: dayGLANCE passed review with the same entitlement and was asked for one.
